The Amarnath Yatra is one of the most sacred pilgrimages dedicated to Lord Shiva, leading devotees to the holy Amarnath Cave in the Himalayas of Jammu & Kashmir. The naturally formed Ice Lingam inside the cave is believed to represent Lord Shiva himself. Undertaken with deep faith and devotion, the Amarnath Yatra is a spiritually powerful journey.
