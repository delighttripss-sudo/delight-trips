Holy Cave · Ice Lingam · Sacred Himalayan Pilgrimage

Talk to Amarnath Yatra Expert

The Amarnath Yatra is among the most revered Hindu pilgrimages, leading devotees to the sacred Amarnath Cave Shrine nestled at an altitude of over 12,700 feet in the Himalayas of Jammu & Kashmir. Inside the cave, the naturally formed Ice Lingam of Lord Shiva appears during the holy Shravan month, symbolising divine cosmic energy.

Undertaken with immense faith and spiritual devotion, the journey to Amarnath is both physically demanding and deeply transformative. Delight Trips offers professionally managed Amarnath Yatra tour packages with proper acclimatisation, experienced ground support, registered permits, medical assistance, and route planning via Pahalgam, Baltal, and Helicopter options.

Why Undertake the Amarnath Yatra?

  • One of the holiest pilgrimages dedicated to Lord Shiva
  • Darshan of the naturally formed Ice Lingam
  • Spiritually powerful Himalayan trekking experience
  • Well-regulated pilgrimage under Shri Amarnathji Shrine Board
  • Options for trekking, assisted yatra, and helicopter darshan
