Talk to Vaishno Devi Yatra Expert

Mata Vaishno Devi, one of the most revered Shakti pilgrimage sites in India, is nestled in the sacred Trikuta Mountains of Jammu. Devotees undertake the holy journey from Katra to the Bhawan with unwavering faith, seeking blessings, fulfillment of wishes, and spiritual strength.

The yatra involves a sacred trek, darshan at the Holy Cave, and the traditional completion at Bhairon Temple. Delight Trips offers professionally managed Vaishno Devi Yatra tour packages with darshan planning, accommodation, optional helicopter services, and complete pilgrimage assistance for a peaceful and dignified spiritual experience.

Why Undertake the Vaishno Devi Yatra?

  • One of the most powerful Shakti Peeth pilgrimages
  • Sacred trek from Katra to the Holy Bhawan
  • Darshan of Mata in the Holy Cave
  • Completion of yatra at Bhairon Temple
  • Options for trekking, ponies, battery car, and helicopter
