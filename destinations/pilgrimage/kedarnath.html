Talk to Kedarnath Yatra Expert

Kedarnath, one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, is among the most revered pilgrimage destinations in India. Located at an altitude of 3,583 meters in the Garhwal Himalayas, Kedarnath is surrounded by snow-clad peaks, ancient legends, and intense spiritual energy.

Reaching the holy shrine involves high-altitude travel and a demanding trek from Gaurikund, making careful planning essential. Delight Trips offers professionally managed Kedarnath Yatra tour packages with proper acclimatisation, guided trek support, comfortable stays, and optional helicopter services.

Why Undertake the Kedarnath Yatra?

  • One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva
  • Spiritually powerful Himalayan pilgrimage
  • Sacred trek from Gaurikund to Kedarnath Temple
  • Mythological significance linked to the Mahabharata
  • Options for trekking and helicopter-assisted darshan
