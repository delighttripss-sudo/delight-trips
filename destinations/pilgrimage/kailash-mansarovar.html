The Kailash Mansarovar Yatra is one of the most sacred and spiritually significant pilgrimages in the world. Revered by Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, and followers of Bon, Mount Kailash is believed to be the eternal abode of Lord Shiva. The sacred Mansarovar Lake symbolizes purity and spiritual awakening. Undertaking this yatra is a profound journey of faith, endurance, and inner transformation.
