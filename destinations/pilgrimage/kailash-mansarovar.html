The Kailash Mansarovar Yatra is one of the most sacred and spiritually significant pilgrimages in the world. Revered by Hindus, Buddhists, Jains, and followers of Bon, Mount Kailash is believed to be the eternal abode of Lord Shiva.

The holy Mansarovar Lake, situated at the base of Mount Kailash, symbolises purity, liberation, and spiritual awakening. Undertaking this yatra involves high-altitude travel, strict medical protocols, and deep spiritual discipline. Delight Trips offers professionally managed Kailash Mansarovar pilgrimage packages via approved Nepal–Tibet routes with complete documentation, acclimatisation planning, and experienced yatra support teams.

Why Undertake the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra?

  • One of the holiest pilgrimages across multiple religions
  • Darshan of Mount Kailash and Mansarovar Lake
  • 108 km sacred parikrama around Mount Kailash
  • Dolma La Pass crossing at 18,600 ft
  • Spiritually transformative once-in-a-lifetime journey
