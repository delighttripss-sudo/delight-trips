The Char Dham Yatra is one of the most sacred pilgrimage journeys in India, encompassing four holy shrines nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas—Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath. Undertaken by devotees seeking spiritual purification and divine blessings, the Char Dham Yatra represents faith, devotion, and inner transformation.
