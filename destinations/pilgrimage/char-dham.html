The Char Dham Yatra is one of the most revered Hindu pilgrimages, covering the four sacred Himalayan shrines of Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath. Located in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand, this divine circuit is believed to cleanse the soul and lead devotees towards spiritual liberation.

Undertaken across high-altitude terrain, river valleys, and ancient temple towns, the Char Dham Yatra requires careful planning, acclimatisation, and experienced ground support. Delight Trips offers professionally curated Char Dham Yatra tour packages with safe road travel, guided assistance, comfortable stays, and optional helicopter yatra services.

Why Undertake the Char Dham Yatra?

  • One of Hinduism’s most sacred pilgrimage circuits
  • Darshan of four holy shrines in the Garhwal Himalayas
  • Spiritually transformative high-altitude journey
  • Ancient temples, sacred rivers, and Himalayan landscapes
  • Options for road-based and helicopter-assisted yatra
