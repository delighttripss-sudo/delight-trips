Nepal is a land of dramatic Himalayan landscapes, ancient temples and deeply rooted spiritual traditions. Nestled between some of the world’s highest peaks, the country offers a rare blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage and peaceful exploration.

Delight Trips offers carefully curated Nepal tour packages designed for leisure travellers, families and explorers. From the cultural heart of Kathmandu and the serene lakes of Pokhara to wildlife reserves and Himalayan viewpoints, our itineraries deliver a balanced and enriching travel experience.

Why Visit Nepal?

  • Majestic Himalayan views and scenic mountain landscapes
  • Ancient temples, monasteries and spiritual landmarks
  • Rich cultural heritage and traditional town centres
  • Wildlife experiences in national parks and reserves
  • Ideal for culture lovers, nature seekers and slow travel
