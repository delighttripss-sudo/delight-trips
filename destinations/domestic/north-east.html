North East India is one of the most culturally rich and visually stunning regions of the country, offering mist-covered hills, pristine forests, crystal-clear rivers and deeply rooted tribal traditions. From living root bridges to serene monasteries, the region promises an immersive and soulful travel experience.

Delight Trips offers thoughtfully curated North East India tour packages covering iconic destinations such as Meghalaya, Assam, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh and hidden gems across the region. Our itineraries are ideal for nature lovers, cultural explorers, photographers and travellers seeking offbeat journeys.

Why Visit North East India?

  • Scenic landscapes including hills, waterfalls and lush valleys
  • Unique tribal cultures, festivals and local traditions
  • Living root bridges, monasteries and spiritual retreats
  • Wildlife experiences in national parks and reserves
  • Ideal for offbeat travel, cultural exploration and nature escapes
