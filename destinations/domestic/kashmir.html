Kashmir, often referred to as Paradise on Earth, is one of India’s most breathtaking destinations, known for its snow-clad mountains, alpine meadows, pristine lakes and timeless natural beauty. From tranquil houseboats in Srinagar to the scenic valleys of Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonmarg, Kashmir offers an unforgettable travel experience in every season.

Delight Trips presents carefully curated Kashmir tour packages for couples, families, honeymooners and adventure seekers. Our itineraries include houseboat stays, shikara rides, snow activities, sightseeing and comfortable transfers—ensuring a safe, seamless and memorable Kashmir holiday.

Why Visit Kashmir?

  • Stay in traditional houseboats on Dal Lake in Srinagar
  • Enjoy snow activities and gondola rides in Gulmarg
  • Explore lush valleys of Pahalgam and Sonmarg
  • Witness seasonal beauty with snow, flowers and autumn colors
  • Experience local Kashmiri culture, cuisine and handicrafts
