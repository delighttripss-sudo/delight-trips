Himachal Pradesh is one of India’s most breathtaking hill destinations, celebrated for its snow-capped Himalayan ranges, lush valleys, alpine forests and serene spiritual towns. From charming hill stations to high-altitude desert landscapes, Himachal offers a perfect escape for nature lovers, adventure seekers and honeymooners alike.

Delight Trips presents carefully curated Himachal Pradesh tour packages covering iconic destinations such as Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Spiti Valley and hidden gems across the Himalayas. Our itineraries are ideal for couples, families, adventure enthusiasts and travellers seeking tranquillity amidst nature.

Why Visit Himachal Pradesh?

  • Famous hill stations including Shimla, Manali, Dalhousie and Kufri
  • Snow experiences, scenic mountain drives and Himalayan views
  • Adventure activities like paragliding, river rafting and trekking
  • Spiritual destinations such as Dharamshala and ancient monasteries
  • Perfect for honeymoon trips, family holidays and adventure tours
