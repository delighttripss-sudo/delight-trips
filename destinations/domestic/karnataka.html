Karnataka is one of India’s most diverse travel destinations, offering a rich blend of ancient heritage, lush coffee plantations, vibrant cities and pristine wildlife reserves. From UNESCO-listed ruins to misty hill stations, the state presents a perfect balance of history, nature and culture.

Delight Trips brings you carefully curated Karnataka tour packages covering iconic destinations such as Bangalore, Mysore, Coorg, Hampi and renowned wildlife sanctuaries. Our itineraries are ideal for families, couples, culture enthusiasts and nature lovers seeking authentic South Indian experiences.

Why Visit Karnataka?

  • Historic landmarks including Hampi, Mysore Palace and ancient temples
  • Scenic coffee plantations and hill retreats in Coorg and Chikmagalur
  • Rich wildlife at national parks and forest reserves
  • Vibrant cities blending modern life with deep-rooted traditions
  • Ideal for cultural tours, nature escapes and heritage holidays
