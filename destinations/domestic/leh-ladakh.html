Leh Ladakh is one of India’s most extraordinary travel destinations, renowned for its dramatic Himalayan landscapes, crystal-clear alpine lakes, ancient Buddhist monasteries and iconic high-altitude mountain passes. This remote region offers an unmatched blend of adventure, spirituality and raw natural beauty.

Delight Trips offers expertly curated Leh Ladakh tour packages covering iconic attractions such as Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, Tso Moriri, historic monasteries and scenic Himalayan routes. Our itineraries are ideal for adventure seekers, photographers, bikers and travellers seeking a truly unforgettable experience.

Why Visit Leh Ladakh?

  • World-famous lakes including Pangong Lake and Tso Moriri
  • Scenic Nubra Valley with sand dunes and monasteries
  • Ancient Buddhist monasteries and spiritual heritage
  • High-altitude passes and dramatic Himalayan landscapes
  • Perfect for adventure tours, road trips and photography
