Kerala, known as God’s Own Country, is one of India’s most beautiful and diverse travel destinations. Famous for its tranquil backwaters, misty hill stations, palm-lined beaches, wildlife sanctuaries and rich cultural heritage, Kerala offers unforgettable experiences for families, couples, honeymooners and nature lovers.

Delight Trips offers carefully curated Kerala tour packages that combine comfort, authentic local experiences and flexible itineraries. From houseboat stays in Alleppey and tea gardens of Munnar to spice plantations, Ayurveda retreats and coastal escapes, our Kerala packages are designed to suit every travel style and budget.

Why Visit Kerala?

  • World-famous backwaters and houseboat experiences
  • Cool hill stations like Munnar and Wayanad
  • Pristine beaches at Kovalam, Varkala and Marari
  • Wildlife sanctuaries and national parks
  • Ayurveda, culture, festivals and local cuisine
