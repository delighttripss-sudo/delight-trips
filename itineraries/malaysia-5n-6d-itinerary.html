Day-wise Malaysia Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kuala Lumpur

Arrive at Kuala Lumpur International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free for leisure or exploring nearby malls and cafés.

Day 2 – Kuala Lumpur City Tour

Half-day guided city tour covering Petronas Twin Towers (photo stop), King’s Palace, National Mosque and Merdeka Square. Afternoon free for shopping at Bukit Bintang or Suria KLCC.

Day 3 – Genting Highlands Excursion

Full-day excursion to Genting Highlands via scenic cable car ride. Enjoy cool mountain air, entertainment zones and leisure time before returning to Kuala Lumpur.

Day 4 – Kuala Lumpur → Langkawi

Check-out and transfer to the airport for your flight to Langkawi. Upon arrival, transfer to your beachside hotel. Evening at leisure.

Day 5 – Leisure Day in Langkawi

Free day to relax on the beach or explore Langkawi at your own pace. Optional activities include island hopping, cable car ride or sunset cruise.

Day 6 – Departure from Langkawi

After breakfast, check-out and transfer to Langkawi Airport for your onward journey. Tour ends with memorable Malaysian experiences.
