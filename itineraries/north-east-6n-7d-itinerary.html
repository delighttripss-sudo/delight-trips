Day-wise North East Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Guwahati & Transfer to Shillong

Arrival at Guwahati airport or railway station. Visit Kamakhya Temple followed by a scenic drive to Shillong. Check-in and overnight stay.

Day 2 – Shillong Local Sightseeing

Explore Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls, Ward’s Lake and Police Bazaar. Evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Shillong to Cherrapunji Excursion

Visit Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave and scenic viewpoints. Return to Shillong by evening.

Day 4 – Shillong to Dawki & Mawlynnong

Day trip to Dawki River known for crystal-clear waters and visit Mawlynnong, Asia’s cleanest village. Return to Shillong.

Day 5 – Shillong to Kaziranga

Scenic drive to Kaziranga National Park. Check-in at wildlife resort and relax.

Day 6 – Kaziranga Safari & Transfer to Guwahati

Early morning jeep safari in Kaziranga followed by transfer to Guwahati. Evening riverfront leisure.

Day 7 – Departure from Guwahati

Transfer to Guwahati airport or railway station. Tour concludes with unforgettable North East memories.
