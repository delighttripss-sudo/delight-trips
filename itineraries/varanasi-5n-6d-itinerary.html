Day-wise Varanasi Pilgrimage Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Varanasi

Arrival in Varanasi. Hotel check-in. Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat - witnessing the mesmerizing fire ritual on the Ganga River. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 2 – Spiritual Morning & Temples

Early morning boat ride on the Ganga. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple - one of the most sacred Shiva temples. Visit Durga Temple and Annapurna Temple. Evening prayers and meditation.

Day 3 – Sarnath Excursion

Day trip to Sarnath - where Buddha delivered his first sermon. Visit Buddhist temples, Dhamek Stupa, and museum. Return to Varanasi for evening Aarti.

Day 4 – Religious Sites & Meditation

Visit Manikarnika Ghat - the primary cremation ghat. Pilgrimage to Annapurna Temple and Kaal Bhairav Temple. Yoga and meditation session. Spiritual discourse.

Day 5 – Prayer & Devotion

Participate in morning prayer sessions at various ashrams. Visit local temples and spiritual centers. Attend evening Aarti. Sacred rituals and blessings.

Day 6 – Final Prayers & Departure

Final morning prayers at Ganga River. Last Aarti attendance. Airport/station drop. Tour concludes with spiritual blessings.
