Day-wise Vietnam Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Hanoi

Arrive at Hanoi International Airport. Meet and greet followed by transfer to hotel. Evening free to explore the Old Quarter or enjoy local street food experiences.

Day 2 – Hanoi City Tour

Guided city tour covering Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (outside view), One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature and Hoan Kiem Lake. Evening free for shopping or optional water puppet show.

Day 3 – Hanoi → Halong Bay Cruise

Transfer to Halong Bay. Board an overnight cruise through limestone karsts. Enjoy kayaking, cave visits, onboard activities and sunset views. Dinner and overnight on cruise.

Day 4 – Halong Bay → Hanoi → Ho Chi Minh City

Morning cruise activities and brunch onboard. Disembark and transfer back to Hanoi for flight to Ho Chi Minh City. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening at leisure.

Day 5 – Ho Chi Minh City & Cu Chi Tunnels

Half-day Cu Chi Tunnels excursion followed by Ho Chi Minh City tour covering War Remnants Museum, Notre Dame Cathedral (outside), Central Post Office and Ben Thanh Market.

Day 6 – Departure

After breakfast, check-out and transfer to Ho Chi Minh City International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes.
