Day-wise Dubai Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Dubai

Arrival at Dubai International Airport. Meet and greet followed by transfer to hotel. Evening free at leisure. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 2 – Dubai City Tour

Guided city tour covering Jumeirah Mosque, Jumeirah Beach, Burj Al Arab photo stop, Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis (outside view), and Gold & Spice Souks.

Day 3 – Desert Safari Experience

Afternoon desert safari with dune bashing, camel ride, sandboarding, cultural shows and BBQ dinner.

Day 4 – Burj Khalifa & Dubai Mall

Visit Burj Khalifa (124th floor – non-prime hours). Explore Dubai Mall and witness Dubai Fountain show.

Day 5 – Abu Dhabi City Tour

Full-day excursion to Abu Dhabi including Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Emirates Palace (photo stop), Corniche and Heritage Village.

Day 6 – Leisure / Optional Tours

Day free for leisure or optional activities such as Miracle Garden, Global Village, Atlantis Aquaventure or Marina Dhow Cruise.

Day 7 – Departure

Transfer to Dubai International Airport for return journey. Tour concludes.
