Day-wise Leh Ladakh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Leh & Acclimatisation

Arrival at Leh airport. Transfer to hotel and complete rest for acclimatisation. Evening visit to Leh Market and Shanti Stupa.

Day 2 – Leh Local Sightseeing

Visit Shey Palace, Thiksey Monastery and Hemis Monastery. Return to hotel for overnight stay.

Day 3 – Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Drive to Nubra Valley via Khardung La, one of the world’s highest motorable passes. Visit Diskit Monastery and Hunder Sand Dunes.

Day 4 – Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake

Travel from Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake via Shyok route. Enjoy stunning landscapes and overnight stay near the lake.

Day 5 – Pangong Lake to Leh

Witness sunrise at Pangong Lake. Drive back to Leh via Chang La Pass. Evening leisure for shopping.

Day 6 – Departure from Leh

Transfer to Leh airport for onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable Himalayan memories.
