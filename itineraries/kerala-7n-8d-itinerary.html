Day-wise Kerala Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kochi

Arrival at Kochi airport or railway station. Transfer to hotel and evening at leisure.

Day 2 – Kochi to Munnar

Scenic drive to Munnar through waterfalls and spice plantations. Overnight stay in Munnar.

Day 3 – Munnar Sightseeing

Visit Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake.

Day 4 – Munnar to Thekkady

Drive to Thekkady. Visit Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary and spice gardens.

Day 5 – Thekkady to Kumarakom

Transfer to Kumarakom. Relax by Vembanad Lake and enjoy resort leisure.

Day 6 – Kumarakom to Alleppey (Houseboat)

Check-in to private houseboat for overnight backwater cruise with meals.

Day 7 – Alleppey to Kovalam

Checkout and proceed to Kovalam beach resort. Evening at leisure.

Day 8 – Departure from Trivandrum

Transfer to Trivandrum airport/railway station. Tour concludes.
