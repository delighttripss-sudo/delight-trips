Day-wise Kerala Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kochi & Transfer to Munnar

Arrival at Kochi airport followed by a scenic drive to Munnar. Overnight stay in Munnar.

Day 2 – Munnar Sightseeing

Visit Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake.

Day 3 – Munnar to Thekkady

Transfer to Thekkady and visit Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary.

Day 4 – Thekkady to Alleppey (Houseboat)

Check-in to private houseboat with backwater cruise and meals.

Day 5 – Alleppey to Kochi

Checkout from houseboat and proceed to Kochi sightseeing.

Day 6 – Departure from Kochi

Airport drop. Tour concludes.
