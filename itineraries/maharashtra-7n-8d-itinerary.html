Day-wise Maharashtra Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Mumbai

Arrival at Mumbai airport or railway station. Visit Gateway of India, Marine Drive and enjoy evening leisure along the coastline.

Day 2 – Mumbai Sightseeing

Visit Siddhivinayak Temple, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (outside view), Elephanta Caves (optional ferry) and Juhu Beach.

Day 3 – Mumbai to Pune

Drive to Pune via the Mumbai–Pune Expressway. Visit Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace and enjoy a relaxed city evening.

Day 4 – Pune to Aurangabad

Travel to Aurangabad by road or overnight train (as preferred). Evening free for rest or local markets.

Day 5 – Ajanta Caves Excursion

Full-day excursion to Ajanta Caves, famous for ancient Buddhist murals and rock-cut architecture.

Day 6 – Ellora Caves & Grishneshwar Temple

Visit Ellora Caves including the Kailasa Temple, followed by darshan at Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga and sightseeing of Bibi Ka Maqbara.

Day 7 – Aurangabad to Mahabaleshwar

Drive to Mahabaleshwar. Evening visit to Venna Lake and local strawberry markets (seasonal).

Day 8 – Mahabaleshwar to Pune / Mumbai Departure

Visit Arthur’s Seat and Wilson Point before driving to Pune or Mumbai for departure. Tour concludes with spiritual and cultural memories of Maharashtra.
