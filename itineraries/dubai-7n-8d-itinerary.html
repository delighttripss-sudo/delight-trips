Day-wise Dubai Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & Evening at Leisure

Arrive at Dubai International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Check-in and relax. Evening free for a nearby walk, café visit, or optional night city views. Overnight stay in Dubai.

Day 2 – Dubai City Tour & Cultural Sights

Guided city tour covering Jumeirah Mosque, Jumeirah Beach, Burj Al Arab photo stop, Palm Jumeirah drive, Atlantis exterior, and traditional Gold & Spice Souks with an Abra ride.

Day 3 – Desert Safari with Entertainment

Afternoon desert safari featuring dune bashing, sandboarding, camel ride, henna painting, live performances and BBQ dinner at a desert camp.

Day 4 – Burj Khalifa & Dubai Mall

Visit Burj Khalifa (124th floor – non-prime hours) for panoramic city views. Explore Dubai Mall, aquarium exterior and enjoy the Dubai Fountain show in the evening.

Day 5 – Abu Dhabi City Tour

Full-day excursion to Abu Dhabi including Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Corniche Drive, Emirates Palace photo stop, Heritage Village and city landmarks before returning to Dubai.

Day 6 – Marina Dhow Cruise Experience

Day free for relaxation or shopping. In the evening, enjoy a Marina Dhow Cruise with international buffet dinner, soft music and stunning skyline views.

Day 7 – Theme Park / Beach Leisure

Choose between a theme park experience (Aquaventure, IMG Worlds or Motiongate) or a relaxed beach day at JBR or La Mer. Evening free for personal exploration.

Day 8 – Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to the airport for your return journey. Tour concludes with lasting memories.
