Day-wise Vietnam Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Hanoi

Arrive at Hanoi International Airport and transfer to hotel. Evening free to explore the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake or enjoy Vietnamese street food. Overnight stay in Hanoi.

Day 2 – Hanoi City Tour

Guided city tour covering Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (outside view), One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature and Hoan Kiem Lake. Evening free or optional water puppet show.

Day 3 – Hanoi → Halong Bay Cruise

Transfer to Halong Bay and board an overnight cruise. Sail through limestone karsts, enjoy kayaking, cave visits, sunset views and onboard activities. Dinner and overnight on cruise.

Day 4 – Halong Bay → Hanoi → Da Nang

Morning cruise activities and brunch. Disembark and transfer back to Hanoi for flight to Da Nang. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening at leisure.

Day 5 – Da Nang City Tour & Marble Mountains

Half-day Da Nang city tour including Marble Mountains, Linh Ung Pagoda, My Khe Beach and Dragon Bridge. Evening free to enjoy beachside cafes.

Day 6 – Da Nang → Hoi An Ancient Town

Transfer to Hoi An. Guided walking tour of Hoi An Ancient Town covering Japanese Covered Bridge, Chinese temples and old merchant houses. Evening lantern-lit streets exploration.

Day 7 – Hoi An Leisure / Optional Experiences

Day free for leisure or optional activities such as basket boat ride, cooking class, cycling through countryside or beach relaxation at An Bang Beach.

Day 8 – Departure

Check-out from hotel and transfer to Da Nang International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes.
