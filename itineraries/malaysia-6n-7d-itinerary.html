Day-wise Malaysia Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kuala Lumpur

Arrive at Kuala Lumpur International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free for leisure or evening shopping at nearby malls.

Day 2 – Kuala Lumpur City Tour

Half-day city tour covering Petronas Twin Towers (photo stop), King’s Palace, National Mosque and Merdeka Square. Afternoon free for shopping or café hopping at Bukit Bintang.

Day 3 – Genting Highlands Day Excursion

Full-day excursion to Genting Highlands with scenic cable car ride. Enjoy entertainment zones and cool climate before returning to Kuala Lumpur by evening.

Day 4 – Kuala Lumpur → Langkawi

Transfer to the airport for your flight to Langkawi. Upon arrival, transfer to your beach resort. Evening at leisure to relax by the sea.

Day 5 – Langkawi Island Leisure

Free day to enjoy Langkawi at your own pace. Optional experiences include island hopping tour, cable car ride or sunset cruise.

Day 6 – Langkawi → Penang

Travel to Penang via domestic flight or ferry (as per schedule). Upon arrival, transfer to hotel. Evening free to explore George Town streets and local cuisine.

Day 7 – Penang City Tour & Departure

Half-day Penang city tour covering George Town heritage areas, temples and local landmarks. Later transfer to Penang Airport for your onward journey.
