Day-wise Maharashtra Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Mumbai

Arrival at Mumbai airport or railway station. Visit Gateway of India, Marine Drive and enjoy evening leisure along the city coastline.

Day 2 – Mumbai Sightseeing

Visit Elephanta Caves (optional ferry), Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (outside view), Siddhivinayak Temple and Juhu Beach.

Day 3 – Mumbai to Lonavala

Drive to Lonavala. Visit Bhushi Dam, Lion’s Point and Rajmachi Viewpoint. Evening at leisure in the hills.

Day 4 – Lonavala to Mahabaleshwar

Scenic drive to Mahabaleshwar. Visit Venna Lake and enjoy sunset at Wilson Point.

Day 5 – Mahabaleshwar Sightseeing & Pune Transfer

Explore Mapro Garden, Arthur’s Seat and Elephant’s Head Point. Later drive to Pune for overnight stay.

Day 6 – Pune Sightseeing & Departure

Visit Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace and local markets. Transfer to Pune or Mumbai airport/railway station for departure.
