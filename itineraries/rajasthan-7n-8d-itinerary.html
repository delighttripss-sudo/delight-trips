Day-wise Rajasthan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Jaipur

Arrival at Jaipur airport or railway station. Check-in to hotel and evening at leisure exploring local handicraft markets.

Day 2 – Jaipur Sightseeing

Visit Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal. Evening cultural program or rooftop dining experience.

Day 3 – Jaipur to Mandawa (Shekhawati Region)

Drive to Mandawa. Explore Mandawa Fort and fresco-painted havelis of Shekhawati region. Overnight heritage stay.

Day 4 – Mandawa to Bikaner

Proceed to Bikaner. Visit Junagarh Fort and National Research Centre on Camel Breeding. Evening at leisure.

Day 5 – Bikaner to Ranthambore

Travel to Ranthambore via scenic countryside routes. Check-in at wildlife resort and relax.

Day 6 – Ranthambore Safari & Transfer to Pushkar

Early morning jungle safari in Ranthambore National Park. Later drive to Pushkar, one of India’s oldest spiritual towns.

Day 7 – Pushkar Sightseeing

Visit Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake and ghats. Free time for spiritual walks or café hopping.

Day 8 – Pushkar to Jaipur Departure

Drive back to Jaipur. Transfer to airport or railway station for onward journey.
