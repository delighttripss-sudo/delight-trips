Day-wise Maharashtra Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Mumbai

Arrival at Mumbai airport or railway station. Visit Gateway of India, Marine Drive and enjoy evening leisure along the coastline.

Day 2 – Mumbai Sightseeing

Explore Elephanta Caves (optional ferry), Siddhivinayak Temple, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (outside view) and Juhu Beach.

Day 3 – Mumbai to Pune

Drive to Pune. Visit Aga Khan Palace, Shaniwar Wada and enjoy a relaxed evening in the city.

Day 4 – Pune to Aurangabad

Travel to Aurangabad by road / train / flight. Evening free to relax or explore local markets.

Day 5 – Ajanta Caves Excursion

Full-day visit to Ajanta Caves, renowned for ancient Buddhist paintings and rock-cut architecture.

Day 6 – Ellora Caves & Aurangabad Sightseeing

Visit Ellora Caves including the Kailasa Temple. Explore Bibi Ka Maqbara and Panchakki.

Day 7 – Departure from Aurangabad / Pune / Mumbai

Transfer to airport or railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes with rich cultural memories of Maharashtra.
