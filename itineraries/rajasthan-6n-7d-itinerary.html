Day-wise Rajasthan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Jaipur

Arrival at Jaipur airport or railway station. Check-in to hotel and evening visit to local bazaars or a traditional Rajasthani dinner.

Day 2 – Jaipur Sightseeing

Visit Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal. Explore Jaipur’s rich heritage and architecture.

Day 3 – Jaipur to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur. Visit Mehrangarh Fort and Jaswant Thada. Evening stroll through the blue city markets.

Day 4 – Jodhpur to Jaisalmer

Scenic drive to Jaisalmer. Visit Jaisalmer Fort and Patwon Ki Haveli. Overnight stay in the desert city.

Day 5 – Jaisalmer Desert Experience

Morning sightseeing followed by camel safari in Sam Sand Dunes. Enjoy sunset views, cultural folk performances and desert camping.

Day 6 – Jaisalmer to Udaipur

Transfer to Udaipur via road/rail/flight (as per plan). Evening leisure by Lake Pichola.

Day 7 – Udaipur Sightseeing & Departure

Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple and enjoy a boat ride on Lake Pichola. Transfer for onward journey.
