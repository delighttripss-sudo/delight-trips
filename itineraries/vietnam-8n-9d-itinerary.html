Day-wise Cambodia – Vietnam Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Hanoi

Arrive at Hanoi International Airport. Transfer to hotel and check-in. Evening free to explore the Old Quarter or enjoy local cuisine.

Day 2 – Hanoi City Tour

Guided city tour including Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (outside view), One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature and Hoan Kiem Lake. Evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Hanoi → Halong Bay Cruise

Transfer to Halong Bay and board an overnight cruise. Enjoy sailing through limestone islands, kayaking, cave visits, sunset views and onboard activities. Dinner and overnight on cruise.

Day 4 – Halong Bay → Hanoi → Ho Chi Minh City

Morning cruise activities and brunch. Disembark and return to Hanoi for flight to Ho Chi Minh City. Arrival and hotel check-in.

Day 5 – Ho Chi Minh City & Cu Chi Tunnels

Half-day Cu Chi Tunnels excursion followed by city tour covering War Remnants Museum, Central Post Office, Notre Dame Cathedral (outside) and Ben Thanh Market.

Day 6 – Ho Chi Minh City → Siem Reap (Cambodia)

Transfer to airport for flight to Siem Reap. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening free to explore local markets or relax.

Day 7 – Angkor Wat Temple Tour

Full-day guided tour of Angkor Wat complex including Angkor Thom, Bayon Temple, Ta Prohm and Angkor Wat. Sunset view at temple complex.

Day 8 – Siem Reap Leisure / Optional Activities

Day free for leisure or optional activities such as Tonle Sap Lake boat ride, Cambodian cultural show or spa treatments.

Day 9 – Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Siem Reap International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes.
