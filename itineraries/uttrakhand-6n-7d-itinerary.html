Day-wise Uttarakhand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & Transfer to Nainital

Arrival at Delhi airport or railway station followed by a scenic drive to Nainital. Evening free for Mall Road walk and lakeside leisure. Overnight stay in Nainital.

Day 2 – Nainital Sightseeing

Visit Naini Lake (boating), Naina Devi Temple, Snow View Point, Tiffin Top and Eco Cave Gardens. Evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Nainital to Kainchi Dham & Mussoorie

Early morning visit to Kainchi Dham Ashram (Neem Karoli Baba). Continue scenic drive to Mussoorie. Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 4 – Mussoorie Sightseeing

Visit Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, Company Garden and Mall Road. Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views. Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 5 – Mussoorie to Rishikesh

Drive to Rishikesh. Visit Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula. Evening attend the soulful Ganga Aarti at Parmarth Niketan. Overnight stay in Rishikesh.

Day 6 – Rishikesh Sightseeing / Leisure

Visit Beatles Ashram, Neelkanth Mahadev Temple (optional), or enjoy yoga sessions and river-side cafés. Optional river rafting (seasonal).

Day 7 – Departure

Drive back to Delhi with serene memories of Uttarakhand. Tour concludes.
