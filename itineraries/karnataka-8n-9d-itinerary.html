Day-wise Karnataka Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bangalore

Arrival at Bangalore airport or railway station. Visit Lalbagh Botanical Garden and Vidhana Soudha (outside view). Overnight stay in Bangalore.

Day 2 – Bangalore to Mysuru

Drive to Mysuru via Srirangapatna. Visit Mysuru Palace, Chamundi Hill and St. Philomena’s Church.

Day 3 – Mysuru to Coorg

Scenic drive to Coorg. Visit Nisargadhama and enjoy leisure time amidst coffee plantations.

Day 4 – Coorg Sightseeing

Explore Dubare Elephant Camp, Abbey Falls, Raja’s Seat and a guided spice plantation walk.

Day 5 – Coorg to Hampi

Long but scenic drive to Hampi. Evening free to relax near Virupaksha Temple area.

Day 6 – Hampi Sightseeing

Full-day exploration of Hampi’s UNESCO monuments including Vittala Temple, Stone Chariot, Lotus Mahal and Elephant Stables.

Day 7 – Hampi to Badami

Drive to Badami. Visit Badami Cave Temples and Agastya Lake. Evening at leisure.

Day 8 – Badami to Udupi

Drive to coastal town of Udupi. Evening visit to Malpe Beach for sunset views and coastal ambience.

Day 9 – Udupi to Bangalore Departure

Early drive back to Bangalore or transfer to nearest convenient airport/railway station. Tour concludes with a complete Karnataka experience.
