Day-wise Thailand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bangkok

Arrival in Bangkok. Hotel check-in. Evening visit to Wat Saket temple and Erawan Shrine. Street food dinner.

Day 2 – Bangkok Cultural Tour

Visit Grand Palace, Wat Phra Kaew, and Wat Pho Temple. Longtail boat tour on Bangkok canals. Traditional massage.

Day 3 – Bangkok to Pattaya

Drive to Pattaya. Beach activities and water sports. Visit Sanctuary of Truth. Pattaya Walking Street shopping.

Day 4 – Pattaya to Phuket

Flight to Phuket. Visit Big Buddha viewpoint. Explore Old Phuket Town with Portuguese architecture.

Day 5 – Phuket Island Adventure

Island hopping tour to Similan Islands. Snorkeling at coral reefs. Beach dinner and night market exploration.

Day 6 – Departure from Phuket

Morning beach leisure. Optional spa treatment. Airport transfer. Tour concludes.
