Uttarakhand 5 Nights 6 Days Hill & Spiritual Tour

Nainital · Kainchi Dham · Mussoorie

Starting from ₹23,999 per person*

Day-wise Uttarakhand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & Transfer to Nainital

Arrival at Delhi railway station or airport followed by a scenic drive to Nainital. Evening free to stroll along Mall Road and enjoy lakeside views. Overnight stay in Nainital.

Day 2 – Nainital Sightseeing

Visit Naini Lake for boating, Naina Devi Temple, Snow View Point, Tiffin Top and Eco Cave Gardens. Evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Nainital to Kainchi Dham & Mussoorie

Early morning visit to Kainchi Dham Ashram (Neem Karoli Baba). Continue drive to Mussoorie via scenic hill roads. Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 4 – Mussoorie Sightseeing

Visit Kempty Falls, Gun Hill Point, Company Garden and Mall Road. Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views. Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 5 – Excursion to Dhanaulti / Leisure

Optional excursion to Dhanaulti Eco Park and Surkanda Devi Temple (subject to time and road conditions) or leisure day in Mussoorie.

Day 6 – Departure

Drive back to Delhi with beautiful memories of Uttarakhand hills. Tour concludes.
