Day-wise Japan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo

Arrive at Tokyo International Airport. Meet our representative and transfer to your hotel. Evening free to explore nearby areas or rest after your journey.

Day 2 – Tokyo City Tour

Guided city tour covering Asakusa Temple, Nakamise Street, Imperial Palace (photo stop), Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower area. Evening free for shopping or local dining.

Day 3 – Mount Fuji & Hakone Day Excursion

Full-day excursion to Mount Fuji area. Visit scenic viewpoints, Lake Ashi and Hakone region (subject to weather). Return to Tokyo by evening.

Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto by Bullet Train

Transfer to station and experience the famous Shinkansen bullet train to Kyoto. Upon arrival, visit Fushimi Inari Shrine and Gion district. Check-in and overnight in Kyoto.

Day 5 – Kyoto & Osaka Sightseeing

Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Proceed to Osaka to explore Dotonbori and Osaka Castle (photo stop). Overnight in Osaka.

Day 6 – Departure from Osaka

After breakfast, transfer to Kansai International Airport for your onward journey. Tour ends with unforgettable Japanese memories.
