Day-wise Bali Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bali & Transfer to Kuta

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport. Meet our local representative and enjoy a private transfer to your hotel in Kuta. Rest of the day is free for leisure or exploring nearby beaches.

Day 2 – Kintamani Volcano & Ubud Cultural Tour

Full-day excursion covering Tegenungan Waterfall, traditional art villages, Kintamani volcano viewpoint, and Ubud attractions including Palace and Art Market. Return to Kuta by evening.

Day 3 – Nusa Penida Island Day Tour

Early transfer to Sanur Harbor for speedboat to Nusa Penida. Visit Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach, Angel’s Billabong and Crystal Bay. Lunch included. Return to Bali in the evening.

Day 4 – Kuta to Ubud | En-route Temples & Rice Terraces

Check-out and transfer to Ubud. En route visit Tirta Empul Holy Spring Temple and Tegallalang Rice Terraces. Check-in and evening at leisure.

Day 5 – Leisure Day in Ubud

Free day to relax or choose optional activities like Balinese spa, yoga session, Monkey Forest visit, cycling tour or café hopping in Ubud town.

Day 6 – Departure from Bali

After breakfast, check-out and transfer to the airport for onward journey. Tour ends with unforgettable memories of Bali.
