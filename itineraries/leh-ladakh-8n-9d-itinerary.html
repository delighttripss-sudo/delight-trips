Day-wise Leh Ladakh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Leh & Acclimatisation

Arrival at Leh airport. Transfer to hotel and complete rest for acclimatisation. Evening visit to Leh Market and Shanti Stupa (subject to health).

Day 2 – Sham Valley Sightseeing

Explore Sham Valley including Magnetic Hill, Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Indus–Zanskar Confluence, Alchi and Lamayuru Monasteries.

Day 3 – Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Drive to Nubra Valley via Khardung La Pass. Visit Diskit Monastery and enjoy camel safari at Hunder Sand Dunes.

Day 4 – Nubra Valley Exploration

Visit Turtuk village (India’s last village near the Pakistan border) or enjoy leisure time in Nubra Valley.

Day 5 – Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake

Travel to Pangong Lake via the Shyok route (subject to conditions). Evening by the lake with overnight stay.

Day 6 – Pangong Lake to Hanle

Drive through the Changthang plateau to Hanle. Visit Hanle Monastery and enjoy stargazing in India’s premier astro region.

Day 7 – Hanle to Tso Moriri

Scenic drive to Tso Moriri Lake via Nyoma and Mahe Bridge. Evening leisure by the pristine lake.

Day 8 – Tso Moriri to Leh

Morning walk near the lake followed by drive back to Leh. Evening at leisure for shopping and café hopping.

Day 9 – Departure from Leh

Transfer to Leh airport for onward journey. Tour concludes with complete Ladakh exploration.
