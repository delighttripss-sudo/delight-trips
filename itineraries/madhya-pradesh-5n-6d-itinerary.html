Day-wise Madhya Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bhopal

Arrival at Bhopal airport or railway station. Visit Upper Lake, Birla Temple and enjoy evening leisure at Van Vihar National Park (outside view).

Day 2 – Bhopal – Sanchi – Bhimbetka

Visit the UNESCO-listed Sanchi Stupa followed by Bhimbetka Rock Shelters, famous for prehistoric cave paintings. Return to Bhopal.

Day 3 – Bhopal to Khajuraho

Transfer to Khajuraho by road / train / flight. Evening free to explore local markets or attend optional light & sound show.

Day 4 – Khajuraho Sightseeing

Explore Western and Eastern Group of Khajuraho Temples, renowned for intricate carvings and UNESCO heritage significance.

Day 5 – Khajuraho to Orchha

Drive to Orchha. Visit Orchha Fort Complex, Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal and Ram Raja Temple.

Day 6 – Orchha to Jhansi Departure

Morning leisure by Betwa River. Transfer to Jhansi railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes with heritage memories of MP.
