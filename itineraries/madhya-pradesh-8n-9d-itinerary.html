Day-wise Madhya Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bhopal

Arrival at Bhopal airport or railway station. Visit Upper Lake, Birla Temple and enjoy evening leisure. Overnight stay in Bhopal.

Day 2 – Bhopal – Sanchi – Ujjain

Visit UNESCO-listed Sanchi Stupa. Continue drive to Ujjain. Evening darshan at Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga.

Day 3 – Ujjain to Omkareshwar

Morning visit to Kal Bhairav Temple. Drive to Omkareshwar. Darshan at Omkareshwar and Mamleshwar temples. Overnight stay.

Day 4 – Omkareshwar to Jabalpur

Drive to Jabalpur. Evening visit to Bhedaghat Marble Rocks and Dhuandhar Falls. Overnight stay in Jabalpur.

Day 5 – Jabalpur to Kanha National Park

Scenic drive to Kanha National Park. Evening at leisure inside the jungle lodge.

Day 6 – Kanha Jungle Safari

Morning and afternoon jeep safaris in Kanha National Park with chances to spot tiger, barasingha and diverse wildlife.

Day 7 – Kanha to Khajuraho

Drive to Khajuraho. Evening free or optional light & sound show at temple complex.

Day 8 – Khajuraho Sightseeing & Orchha

Visit Western and Eastern Group of Khajuraho Temples. Continue drive to Orchha for overnight stay.

Day 9 – Orchha to Jhansi Departure

Visit Orchha Fort Complex and Ram Raja Temple. Transfer to Jhansi railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes.
