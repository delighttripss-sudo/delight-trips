Day-wise Rajasthan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Jaipur

Arrival at Jaipur airport. Hotel check-in. Evening visit to City Palace and Jantar Mantar. Overnight stay in Jaipur.

Day 2 – Jaipur Exploration

Visit Hawa Mahal, Albert Hall Museum, local markets. Elephant ride at Amber Fort with panoramic views.

Day 3 – Jaipur to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur. Visit magnificent Mehrangarh Fort with stunning architecture. Explore Clock Tower and local bazaars.

Day 4 – Jodhpur to Udaipur

Drive to Udaipur. Visit City Palace and Lake Palace. Evening sunset boat ride on Pichola Lake.

Day 5 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Visit Jag Mandir, explore Saheliyon ki Bari garden. Traditional puppet show and cultural performances in evening.

Day 6 – Departure from Udaipur

Morning leisure at lake. Airport or train station drop. Tour concludes.
