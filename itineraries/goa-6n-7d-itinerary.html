Day-wise Goa Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Goa & Check-in

Arrival at Dabolim airport followed by hotel check-in. Evening beach walk at Baga Beach. Overnight stay in Goa.

Day 2 – North Goa Sightseeing

Visit Fort Aguada, Anjuna Beach, Vagator Beach and explore local markets. Water sports activities available.

Day 3 – Spice Plantation & Heritage Tour

Guided tour of spice plantations with tasting session. Visit Old Goa churches and Basilica of Bom Jesus.

Day 4 – South Goa Beach & Adventures

Explore Palolem Beach, Colva Beach. Optional dolphin watching, scuba diving or island hopping adventures.

Day 5 – Adventure Day

Parasailing, jet skiing or banana boat rides. Sunset cruise. Local dining experiences.

Day 6 – Relaxation & Wellness

Ayurvedic spa treatment or beach yoga session. Evening shopping and local market exploration.

Day 7 – Departure from Goa

Leisure morning at beach. Airport drop. Tour concludes.
