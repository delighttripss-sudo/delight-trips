Day-wise Vietnam Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Hanoi

Arrive at Hanoi International Airport. Transfer to hotel and check-in. Evening free to explore the Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake or enjoy Vietnamese cuisine at local cafés.

Day 2 – Hanoi City Tour

Guided city tour covering Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum (outside view), One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature and Hoan Kiem Lake. Evening free or optional water puppet show.

Day 3 – Ninh Binh Day Excursion

Full-day excursion to Ninh Binh featuring Tam Coc or Trang An boat ride through limestone karsts, visits to ancient temples and scenic countryside. Return to Hanoi by evening.

Day 4 – Hanoi → Halong Bay Cruise

Transfer to Halong Bay and board an overnight cruise. Enjoy kayaking, cave exploration, onboard activities and sunset views. Dinner and overnight stay on cruise.

Day 5 – Halong Bay → Hanoi → Ho Chi Minh City

Morning cruise activities followed by brunch. Disembark and transfer back to Hanoi for flight to Ho Chi Minh City. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening at leisure.

Day 6 – Ho Chi Minh City & Cu Chi Tunnels

Half-day Cu Chi Tunnels excursion followed by city tour covering War Remnants Museum, Central Post Office, Notre Dame Cathedral (outside) and Ben Thanh Market.

Day 7 – Departure

After breakfast, check-out and transfer to Ho Chi Minh City International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes.
