Luxury Maldives Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Maldives & Seaplane Transfer

Arrive at Velana International Airport, Male. Enjoy VIP assistance followed by a scenic seaplane transfer to your luxury private island resort. Check-in and relax amidst turquoise waters.

Day 2 – Leisure & Island Indulgence

Enjoy a full leisure day exploring the island, infinity pools, white-sand beaches and resort facilities. Perfect day to unwind after travel.

Day 3 – Snorkeling & Marine Exploration

Guided snorkeling experience at the house reef to discover vibrant coral gardens and marine life. Afternoon free for spa or private beach relaxation.

Day 4 – Transition to Overwater Villa

Upgrade and shift to your overwater villa. Spend the day enjoying direct lagoon access, glass-floor views and unmatched privacy.

Day 5 – All-Inclusive Luxury & Sunset Cruise

Indulge in gourmet dining across resort restaurants. Evening features a luxury sunset cruise with panoramic Indian Ocean views.

Day 6 – Ultimate Leisure & Wellness

A slow luxury day to enjoy spa treatments, yoga sessions, kayaking or simply relaxing in your overwater villa with uninterrupted ocean views.

Day 7 – Private Beach Time & Farewell Dinner

Enjoy your final full day with private beach moments, swimming and resort leisure. Optional farewell beachfront dining experience can be arranged.

Day 8 – Departure from Maldives

After breakfast, seaplane transfer back to Male International Airport for your onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable luxury island memories.
