Day-wise Japan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo

Arrive at Tokyo International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free to relax or explore nearby neighborhoods.

Day 2 – Tokyo City Tour

Full-day Tokyo sightseeing covering Asakusa Temple, Nakamise Street, Imperial Palace area (photo stop), Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower surroundings. Evening free for leisure.

Day 3 – Mount Fuji & Hakone Excursion

Day trip to Mount Fuji region and Hakone. Enjoy scenic viewpoints, Lake Ashi and surrounding natural landscapes (weather permitting). Return to Tokyo by evening.

Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto by Bullet Train

Experience the world-famous Shinkansen bullet train to Kyoto. Afternoon sightseeing includes Fushimi Inari Shrine and Gion district. Overnight in Kyoto.

Day 5 – Kyoto & Nara Day Tour

Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Continue to Nara to explore Todaiji Temple and Nara Deer Park before returning to Kyoto.

Day 6 – Kyoto → Osaka | City Highlights

Transfer to Osaka. Visit Osaka Castle (photo stop) and explore Dotonbori district, known for its vibrant nightlife and street food. Overnight in Osaka.

Day 7 – Departure from Osaka

After breakfast, transfer to Kansai International Airport for your onward journey. Tour concludes with lasting memories of Japan.
