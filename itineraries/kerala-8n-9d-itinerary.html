Day-wise Kerala Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kochi

Arrival at Kochi airport or railway station. Transfer to hotel and evening at leisure.

Day 2 – Kochi to Munnar

Scenic drive to Munnar through waterfalls and spice plantations. Overnight stay in Munnar.

Day 3 – Munnar Sightseeing

Visit Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point and Kundala Lake.

Day 4 – Munnar to Thekkady

Drive to Thekkady. Visit Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary and spice plantations.

Day 5 – Thekkady to Vagamon

Transfer to Vagamon. Explore meadows, pine forests and scenic viewpoints.

Day 6 – Vagamon to Alleppey (Houseboat)

Drive to Alleppey and check-in to a private houseboat with overnight backwater cruise and meals.

Day 7 – Alleppey to Kovalam

Checkout from houseboat and proceed to Kovalam beach resort. Evening at leisure.

Day 8 – Kovalam & Trivandrum Sightseeing

Relax at Kovalam Beach or explore Trivandrum attractions such as Padmanabhaswamy Temple and local markets.

Day 9 – Departure from Trivandrum

Transfer to Trivandrum airport or railway station. Tour concludes.
