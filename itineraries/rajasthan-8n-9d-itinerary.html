Day-wise Rajasthan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Udaipur

Arrival at Udaipur airport or railway station. Check-in and evening boat ride on Lake Pichola with sunset views.

Day 2 – Udaipur Sightseeing

Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon Ki Bari and explore heritage streets of old Udaipur.

Day 3 – Udaipur to Kumbhalgarh

Drive to Kumbhalgarh Fort, home to the second-longest wall in the world. Evening light & sound show.

Day 4 – Kumbhalgarh to Mount Abu

Travel to Mount Abu, Rajasthan’s only hill station. Sunset at Nakki Lake and leisure evening.

Day 5 – Mount Abu Sightseeing

Visit Dilwara Jain Temples, Guru Shikhar and local viewpoints. Enjoy relaxed hill station vibes.

Day 6 – Mount Abu to Banswara

Drive to Banswara, known as the “City of Hundred Islands”. Explore tribal villages and scenic lakes.

Day 7 – Banswara to Chittorgarh

Proceed to Chittorgarh. Visit the iconic Chittorgarh Fort, Vijay Stambh and Rani Padmini Palace.

Day 8 – Chittorgarh to Bundi

Travel to Bundi. Explore Taragarh Fort, stepwells and blue houses of this charming heritage town.

Day 9 – Bundi to Jaipur Departure

Drive to Jaipur for onward journey. Tour concludes with deep cultural and natural Rajasthan experiences.
