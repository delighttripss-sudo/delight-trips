Day-wise Malaysia Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Kuala Lumpur

Arrive at Kuala Lumpur International Airport. Meet our local representative and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free for relaxation or exploring nearby markets.

Day 2 – Kuala Lumpur City Tour

Half-day guided city tour covering Petronas Twin Towers (photo stop), King’s Palace, National Mosque and Independence Square. Afternoon free for shopping at Bukit Bintang or Suria KLCC.

Day 3 – Genting Highlands Excursion

Full-day excursion to Genting Highlands via scenic cable car ride. Enjoy the cool climate, entertainment zones and leisure time. Return to Kuala Lumpur by evening.

Day 4 – Leisure Day in Kuala Lumpur

Day free to explore Kuala Lumpur at your own pace. Optional visits include Batu Caves, local street food trails or premium outlet shopping.

Day 5 – Departure from Malaysia

After breakfast, check-out and transfer to the airport for your onward journey. Tour ends with memorable Malaysian experiences.
