Day-wise Madhya Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bhopal

Arrival at Bhopal airport or railway station. Visit Upper Lake, Birla Temple and enjoy a relaxed evening. Overnight stay in Bhopal.

Day 2 – Bhopal – Sanchi – Ujjain

Visit the UNESCO-listed Sanchi Stupa in the morning. Continue drive to Ujjain. Evening visit to Mahakaleshwar Temple for darshan.

Day 3 – Ujjain to Omkareshwar

Morning visit to Kal Bhairav Temple. Drive to Omkareshwar. Visit Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga and Mamleshwar Temple. Overnight stay.

Day 4 – Omkareshwar to Khajuraho

Transfer to Khajuraho by road/train/flight (via Indore as required). Evening at leisure or optional cultural show.

Day 5 – Khajuraho Sightseeing

Explore Western and Eastern Group of Khajuraho Temples, globally renowned for intricate carvings and UNESCO heritage value.

Day 6 – Khajuraho to Orchha

Drive to Orchha. Visit Orchha Fort Complex, Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal and Ram Raja Temple.

Day 7 – Orchha to Jhansi Departure

Morning leisure by the Betwa River. Transfer to Jhansi railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes with spiritual and heritage memories of MP.
