Day-wise Uttar Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Agra

Arrival at Agra railway station or via Delhi. Visit the Taj Mahal (UNESCO World Heritage Site) followed by Agra Fort. Evening at leisure exploring local markets.

Day 2 – Agra to Lucknow

Morning drive to Lucknow. En route optional stop at Fatehpur Sikri. Evening free to experience Awadhi cuisine.

Day 3 – Lucknow Sightseeing

Visit Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara, Rumi Darwaza and Residency. Evening stroll at Gomti Riverfront.

Day 4 – Lucknow to Varanasi

Travel to Varanasi by road/train/flight. Evening attend the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Day 5 – Varanasi Sightseeing

Early morning boat ride on River Ganga. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sarnath (Buddhist site) and explore ancient city lanes.

Day 6 – Departure from Varanasi

Transfer to Varanasi airport or railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes with spiritual and cultural memories of Uttar Pradesh.
