Day-wise Leh Ladakh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Leh & Acclimatisation

Arrival at Leh airport. Transfer to hotel and complete rest for acclimatisation. Evening visit to Leh Market and Shanti Stupa if health permits.

Day 2 – Leh Local Sightseeing

Visit Shey Palace, Thiksey Monastery and Hemis Monastery. Relaxed sightseeing to aid altitude adjustment.

Day 3 – Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Drive to Nubra Valley via Khardung La Pass. Visit Diskit Monastery and enjoy camel safari at Hunder Sand Dunes.

Day 4 – Nubra Valley to Pangong Lake

Travel to Pangong Lake via Shyok route (subject to road conditions). Evening at leisure by the lake with overnight stay.

Day 5 – Pangong Lake to Tso Moriri

Scenic drive through the Changthang plateau via Nyoma and Mahe Bridge. Reach Tso Moriri, one of Ladakh’s most pristine lakes.

Day 6 – Tso Moriri to Leh

Morning walk near the lake followed by drive back to Leh via Mahe and Sumdo villages. Evening rest.

Day 7 – Leh Leisure / Optional Activities

Free day for shopping, café hopping, or optional rafting on the Indus River. Relaxed pace before departure.

Day 8 – Departure from Leh

Transfer to Leh airport for onward journey. Tour concludes with deep Himalayan and remote Ladakh experiences.
