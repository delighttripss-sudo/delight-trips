Day-wise Thailand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Phuket → Transfer to Krabi

Arrive at Phuket International Airport. Meet and greet followed by scenic road transfer to Krabi. Check-in at hotel and evening free to relax at Ao Nang Beach or explore local markets.

Day 2 – Krabi Four Island Tour

Full-day Four Island tour by speedboat covering Phra Nang Cave, Chicken Island, Tup Island and Poda Island. Enjoy snorkeling, beach time and a packed lunch. Evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Krabi Leisure / Optional Experiences

Day free to relax or explore Railay Beach, Emerald Pool, Hot Springs or enjoy a Thai spa treatment. Ideal for slow-paced island relaxation.

Day 4 – Krabi → Phuket Transfer

After breakfast, transfer from Krabi to Phuket by road. Hotel check-in and evening free to explore Patong Beach or enjoy a beachfront dinner.

Day 5 – Phi Phi Island Tour

Full-day Phi Phi Island tour by speedboat including Maya Bay, Monkey Beach, snorkeling spots and scenic lagoons. Lunch included. Return to Phuket by evening.

Day 6 – Phuket City Tour

Half-day Phuket city tour covering Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, Karon Viewpoint and Phuket Old Town. Evening free for shopping, nightlife or optional Fantasea Show.

Day 7 – Leisure Day in Phuket

Day free for beach relaxation, island hopping add-ons, scuba diving, sunset cruise or simply enjoying resort facilities.

Day 8 – Departure

Check-out from hotel and transfer to Phuket International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable island memories.
