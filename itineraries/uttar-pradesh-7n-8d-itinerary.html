Day-wise Uttar Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Mathura

Arrival via Delhi. Visit Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi Temple and Dwarkadhish Temple. Evening free at leisure. Overnight stay in Mathura.

Day 2 – Mathura & Vrindavan Sightseeing

Visit Banke Bihari Temple, ISKCON Temple, Prem Mandir and Nidhivan. Evening aarti at Prem Mandir. Overnight stay in Mathura/Vrindavan.

Day 3 – Mathura to Agra

Drive to Agra. Visit the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort. Evening free for local market exploration.

Day 4 – Agra to Lucknow

Drive to Lucknow with optional stop at Fatehpur Sikri. Evening free to enjoy Awadhi cuisine.

Day 5 – Lucknow Sightseeing & Ayodhya Transfer

Visit Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara and Rumi Darwaza. Later drive to Ayodhya. Evening visit to Ram Janmabhoomi Temple.

Day 6 – Ayodhya to Prayagraj

Visit Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan. Drive to Prayagraj. Visit Triveni Sangam and Anand Bhawan.

Day 7 – Prayagraj to Varanasi

Morning boat ride at Sangam (seasonal). Drive to Varanasi. Evening attend the grand Ganga Aarti.

Day 8 – Varanasi Sightseeing & Departure

Early morning boat ride on the Ganges. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Sarnath. Transfer to airport or railway station for departure.
