Day-wise Uttar Pradesh Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Agra

Arrival at Agra via Delhi or railway station. Visit the Taj Mahal (UNESCO World Heritage Site) and Agra Fort. Evening free at leisure.

Day 2 – Agra to Lucknow

Drive to Lucknow with an optional stop at Fatehpur Sikri. Evening free to enjoy authentic Awadhi cuisine.

Day 3 – Lucknow Sightseeing & Ayodhya Transfer

Visit Bara Imambara, Chota Imambara and Rumi Darwaza. Later drive to Ayodhya. Evening visit to Ram Janmabhoomi Temple.

Day 4 – Ayodhya to Prayagraj

Visit Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan in Ayodhya. Drive to Prayagraj and witness Triveni Sangam and Anand Bhawan.

Day 5 – Prayagraj to Varanasi

Morning boat ride at Sangam (seasonal). Drive to Varanasi. Evening attend the spectacular Ganga Aarti.

Day 6 – Varanasi Sightseeing

Early morning boat ride on the Ganges. Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sarnath and explore the ancient lanes of Varanasi.

Day 7 – Departure from Varanasi

Transfer to Varanasi airport or railway station. Tour concludes with profound spiritual and cultural experiences.
