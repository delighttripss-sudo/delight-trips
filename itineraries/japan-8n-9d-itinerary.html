Day-wise Japan & South Korea Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo

Arrive at Tokyo International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free to relax or explore nearby neighborhoods.

Day 2 – Tokyo City Tour

Full-day Tokyo sightseeing covering Asakusa Temple, Nakamise Street, Imperial Palace area (photo stop), Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower surroundings. Evening free for shopping or dining.

Day 3 – Mount Fuji & Hakone Excursion

Scenic excursion to Mount Fuji and Hakone region including Lake Ashi and surrounding viewpoints (subject to weather). Return to Tokyo by evening.

Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto by Bullet Train

Travel by Shinkansen bullet train to Kyoto. Afternoon visit to Fushimi Inari Shrine and stroll through Gion district. Overnight in Kyoto.

Day 5 – Kyoto & Osaka Highlights

Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Proceed to Osaka to explore Osaka Castle (photo stop) and Dotonbori. Overnight in Osaka.

Day 6 – Osaka → Seoul (Flight)

Transfer to airport for flight to Seoul, South Korea. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening free to explore Myeongdong or local cafés.

Day 7 – Seoul City Tour

Guided city tour covering Gyeongbokgung Palace, Bukchon Hanok Village, Blue House (photo stop) and Insadong cultural street.

Day 8 – Seoul → Busan

Travel to Busan by high-speed KTX train. Visit Haeundae Beach and Jagalchi Fish Market. Evening at leisure.

Day 9 – Departure from Busan

After breakfast, transfer to Busan International Airport for your onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable East Asian memories.
