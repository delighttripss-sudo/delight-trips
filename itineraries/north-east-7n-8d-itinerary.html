Day-wise North East Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Guwahati

Arrival at Guwahati airport or railway station. Visit Kamakhya Temple followed by hotel check-in. Evening Brahmaputra riverfront leisure.

Day 2 – Guwahati to Kaziranga

Scenic drive to Kaziranga National Park. Check-in at wildlife resort and evening at leisure.

Day 3 – Kaziranga Safari & Transfer to Shillong

Early morning jeep safari in Kaziranga. Post breakfast drive to Shillong via scenic Assam–Meghalaya landscapes.

Day 4 – Shillong & Cherrapunji Excursion

Visit Nohkalikai Falls, Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave and viewpoints. Return to Shillong by evening.

Day 5 – Shillong to Bhalukpong to Bomdila

Drive towards Arunachal Pradesh via Bhalukpong. Enter Arunachal with inner-line permit assistance. Proceed to Bomdila for overnight stay.

Day 6 – Bomdila to Dirang

Visit Bomdila Monastery and scenic viewpoints. Continue to Dirang Valley, known for hot springs and apple orchards.

Day 7 – Dirang to Guwahati

Long but scenic return drive to Guwahati via Assam plains. Evening leisure and rest.

Day 8 – Departure from Guwahati

Transfer to Guwahati airport or railway station. Tour concludes with diverse North East experiences.
