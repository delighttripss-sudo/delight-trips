Uttarakhand 8 Nights 9 Days Complete Nature & Hills Tour

Day-wise Uttarakhand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & Transfer to Jim Corbett

Arrival at Delhi airport or railway station followed by drive to Jim Corbett National Park. Evening free to relax in forest surroundings. Overnight stay near Corbett.

Day 2 – Jim Corbett Jungle Safari

Early morning jeep safari inside Jim Corbett National Park (subject to permit availability). Afternoon leisure at resort.

Day 3 – Corbett to Nainital

Drive to Nainital. Evening free to explore Mall Road and enjoy lakeside ambience.

Day 4 – Nainital Sightseeing

Visit Naini Lake (boating), Snow View Point, Tiffin Top and Eco Cave Gardens. Leisure evening.

Day 5 – Nainital to Ranikhet

Scenic drive to Ranikhet. Visit Chaubatia Gardens, Jhula Devi Temple surroundings and enjoy peaceful hill views.

Day 6 – Ranikhet to Kausani

Drive to Kausani, known for panoramic Himalayan ranges. Evening free for sunset views over Nanda Devi and Trishul peaks.

Day 7 – Kausani to Mussoorie

Drive to Mussoorie through scenic mountain roads. Evening free to stroll on Mall Road.

Day 8 – Mussoorie to Rishikesh

Visit Kempty Falls and Gun Hill before driving to Rishikesh. Evening leisure near river cafés and suspension bridges.

Day 9 – Rishikesh Leisure & Departure

Morning free for yoga, riverside walks or optional rafting (seasonal). Drive back to Delhi. Tour concludes.
