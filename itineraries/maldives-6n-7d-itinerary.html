Day-wise Maldives Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Maldives & Resort Transfer

Arrive at Velana International Airport, Male. Meet resort representative and transfer to your island resort via speedboat or seaplane (as per resort location). Check-in and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure.

Day 2 – Leisure Day at Resort

Enjoy a relaxed day exploring the island, beach walks, swimming in crystal-clear waters or simply unwinding at your villa. Optional water sports available.

Day 3 – Snorkeling & Ocean Activities

Experience guided snorkeling in the house reef or lagoon. Discover vibrant coral reefs and marine life. Afternoon free for leisure or spa sessions.

Day 4 – Overwater Villa Stay

Upgrade or shift to an overwater villa (subject to package). Spend the day enjoying direct lagoon access, panoramic ocean views and unmatched privacy.

Day 5 – Leisure & Sunset Experience

Free day to relax. Enjoy sunset views, beachside dining or optional sunset cruise experience arranged by the resort.

Day 6 – Island Leisure & Resort Activities

Final full day to enjoy resort facilities such as kayaking, paddle boarding, spa or simply relaxing by the infinity pool.

Day 7 – Departure from Maldives

After breakfast, transfer back to Male International Airport for your onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable island memories.
