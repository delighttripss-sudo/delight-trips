Day-wise North East Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Guwahati

Arrival at Guwahati airport or railway station. Evening Brahmaputra riverfront walk and overnight stay.

Day 2 – Guwahati to Dimapur & Transfer to Kohima

Morning flight/train to Dimapur. Drive to Kohima via scenic hill roads. Check-in and rest.

Day 3 – Kohima Sightseeing

Visit Kohima War Cemetery, State Museum and local Naga villages. Evening cultural interaction.

Day 4 – Dzükou Valley Excursion

Full-day excursion to Dzükou Valley known for rolling meadows and seasonal flowers. Return to Kohima by evening.

Day 5 – Kohima to Dimapur & Fly to Aizawl

Drive back to Dimapur and take a connecting flight to Aizawl. Evening leisure in Mizoram’s capital.

Day 6 – Aizawl Sightseeing

Explore Solomon’s Temple, Mizoram State Museum, local markets and viewpoints overlooking lush valleys.

Day 7 – Aizawl to Reiek / Hmuifang

Day trip to Reiek Heritage Village or Hmuifang Hills. Experience traditional Mizo culture and landscapes.

Day 8 – Aizawl to Guwahati

Flight back to Guwahati. Final shopping and leisure evening.

Day 9 – Departure from Guwahati

Transfer to Guwahati airport or railway station. Tour concludes with rare North East cultural experiences.
