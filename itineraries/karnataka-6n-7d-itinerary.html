Day-wise Karnataka Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bangalore

Arrival at Bangalore airport or railway station. Visit Lalbagh Botanical Garden and enjoy a relaxed city evening.

Day 2 – Bangalore to Mysuru

Drive to Mysuru via Srirangapatna. Visit Mysuru Palace, Chamundi Hill and St. Philomena’s Church.

Day 3 – Mysuru to Coorg

Scenic drive to Coorg. Visit Nisargadhama and enjoy leisure time amidst coffee plantations.

Day 4 – Coorg Sightseeing

Visit Dubare Elephant Camp, Abbey Falls, Raja’s Seat and a local spice plantation.

Day 5 – Coorg to Chikmagalur

Drive to Chikmagalur. Visit Mullayanagiri viewpoint and enjoy sunset views over the Western Ghats.

Day 6 – Chikmagalur Sightseeing

Explore Baba Budangiri, coffee estates and serene hill landscapes. Evening at leisure.

Day 7 – Chikmagalur to Bangalore Departure

Drive back to Bangalore. Transfer to airport or railway station. Tour concludes with memorable Karnataka experiences.
