Day-wise Thailand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bangkok → Transfer to Pattaya

Arrive at Bangkok International Airport. Meet and greet followed by scenic road transfer to Pattaya. Check-in at hotel and evening free to explore Pattaya Beach or Walking Street.

Day 2 – Coral Island Tour (Pattaya)

Speedboat excursion to Coral Island with crystal-clear waters. Optional water sports like parasailing or jet ski. Return to hotel and evening enjoy Alcazar Show or local nightlife.

Day 3 – Pattaya → Bangkok | City Tour

Transfer back to Bangkok. Guided city tour covering Golden Buddha, Marble Temple and Gems Gallery. Evening free for shopping at MBK Mall or night markets.

Day 4 – Bangkok → Phuket (Flight)

Transfer to airport for domestic flight to Phuket (own arrangement or optional add-on). Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening at leisure to relax at Patong Beach.

Day 5 – Phi Phi Island Tour

Full-day Phi Phi Island tour by speedboat covering Maya Bay, Monkey Beach and snorkeling spots. Lunch included. Return by evening and overnight stay in Phuket.

Day 6 – Phuket City Tour & Leisure

Half-day Phuket city tour including Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, viewpoint stops and Old Town. Evening free for shopping, spa or optional sunset cruise.

Day 7 – Departure

Check-out from hotel and transfer to Phuket International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes.
