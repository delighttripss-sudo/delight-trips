Luxury Bali Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bali & Private Pool Villa Experience

Arrive in Bali and enjoy VIP meet-and-greet followed by private transfer to your luxury pool villa in Ubud outskirts. Evening free to unwind in privacy and nature.

Day 2 – Leisure & Wellness Indulgence

A relaxed day to enjoy your private villa, infinity pool and surroundings. Optional wellness spa or floating breakfast experience can be arranged.

Day 3 – Ubud Cultural & Scenic Tour

Visit Tegallalang Rice Terraces, Tirta Empul Holy Spring Temple and Ubud town. Return early to enjoy villa leisure and sunset views.

Day 4 – North Bali Luxury Exploration

Full-day excursion through North Bali covering scenic mountain roads, waterfalls, and lake viewpoints. A serene and exclusive experience away from crowds.

Day 5 – Nusa Penida Premium Island Escape

Early transfer to Sanur Harbor for premium speedboat to Nusa Penida. Explore iconic beaches including Kelingking, Angel’s Billabong and Crystal Bay with curated stops and lunch.

Day 6 – Transfer to South Bali Luxury Beach Resort

Check-out and proceed to a luxury beach or cliffside resort in South Bali. Evening free to enjoy ocean views and resort amenities.

Day 7 – South Bali Coastline & Uluwatu Sunset

Explore Melasti Beach and Uluwatu Temple. Witness a dramatic sunset overlooking the Indian Ocean. Optional Kecak dance experience.

Day 8 – Seminyak Leisure & Premium Shopping

Leisure day to relax by the beach or explore Seminyak’s boutique shopping, cafés and beach clubs. A perfect blend of luxury and lifestyle.

Day 9 – Departure from Bali

After breakfast, private transfer to the airport. Your luxury Bali journey concludes with unforgettable experiences.
