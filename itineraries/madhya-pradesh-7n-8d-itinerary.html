Day-wise Madhya Pradesh Wildlife Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Jabalpur

Arrival at Jabalpur airport or railway station. Visit Marble Rocks at Bhedaghat and Dhuandhar Falls. Overnight stay in Jabalpur.

Day 2 – Jabalpur to Kanha National Park

Scenic drive to Kanha National Park. Evening at leisure inside the forest lodge to soak in jungle surroundings.

Day 3 – Kanha Jungle Safaris

Morning and afternoon jeep safaris in Kanha National Park with chances to spot tiger, barasingha, deer and rich birdlife.

Day 4 – Kanha to Bandhavgarh National Park

Drive to Bandhavgarh National Park, known for one of the highest tiger densities in India. Evening free to relax.

Day 5 – Bandhavgarh Jungle Safaris

Morning and afternoon jeep safaris in Bandhavgarh National Park. Explore core zones with expert naturalists.

Day 6 – Bandhavgarh to Khajuraho

Drive to Khajuraho. Evening free to explore local markets or attend optional light & sound show at temples.

Day 7 – Khajuraho Sightseeing

Visit Western and Eastern Group of Khajuraho Temples, renowned for intricate carvings and UNESCO heritage status.

Day 8 – Departure from Khajuraho

Transfer to Khajuraho airport or railway station for onward journey. Tour concludes with unforgettable wildlife memories.
