Your Signature Bali Honeymoon Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Bali & Private Pool Villa Check-in

Arrive in Bali and enjoy a private transfer to your honeymoon private pool villa in Ubud outskirts. Floral welcome and time to relax in complete privacy.

Day 2 – Leisure & Couples Spa Experience

A relaxed honeymoon day. Enjoy your private pool and later indulge in a romantic couples spa session at a premium wellness retreat. Evening free for villa dining.

Day 3 – Ubud Cultural & Scenic Exploration

Visit Tegallalang Rice Terraces, Tirta Empul Holy Spring Temple and Ubud town. Return to your villa by afternoon to enjoy leisure time.

Day 4 – North Bali Discovery Tour

Full-day excursion to North Bali covering Banyumala or Sekumpul Waterfalls, scenic mountain roads and Lake viewpoints. A serene, less-crowded romantic experience.

Day 5 – Transfer to South Bali & Romantic Evening

Check-out and proceed to South Bali beach resort. Evening features a specially arranged romantic dining experience by the beach or resort setting.

Day 6 – Leisure Day at Beach Resort

Enjoy a slow honeymoon day by the beach or resort pool. Optional photoshoot, water activities or sunset walk along the coast.

Day 7 – Uluwatu Coast & Cliffside Views

Visit Uluwatu Temple and nearby coastal viewpoints. Witness dramatic ocean vistas and sunset. Evening free for shopping or resort leisure.

Day 8 – Departure from Bali

After breakfast, private transfer to the airport. Your honeymoon concludes with memories of a truly intimate Bali escape.
