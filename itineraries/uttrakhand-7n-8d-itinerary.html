Uttarakhand 7 Nights 8 Days Wildlife & Hill Station Tour

Jim Corbett · Nainital · Mussoorie · Rishikesh

Starting from ₹31,999 per person*

Day-wise Uttarakhand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & Transfer to Jim Corbett

Arrival at Delhi railway station or airport followed by drive to Jim Corbett National Park. Evening at leisure amidst forest surroundings. Overnight stay near Corbett.

Day 2 – Jim Corbett Jungle Safari

Early morning jeep safari inside Jim Corbett National Park (subject to availability). Afternoon free for relaxation or nature walks near the resort.

Day 3 – Corbett to Nainital

Drive to Nainital. Evening free to explore Mall Road and enjoy lakeside views. Overnight stay in Nainital.

Day 4 – Nainital Sightseeing

Visit Naini Lake (boating), Naina Devi Temple, Snow View Point, Tiffin Top and Eco Cave Gardens.

Day 5 – Nainital to Mussoorie

Scenic drive to Mussoorie via hill roads. Evening free to stroll on Mall Road. Overnight stay in Mussoorie.

Day 6 – Mussoorie Sightseeing

Visit Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, Company Garden and enjoy panoramic Himalayan views.

Day 7 – Mussoorie to Rishikesh

Drive to Rishikesh. Visit Ram Jhula and Laxman Jhula. Evening attend Ganga Aarti at Parmarth Niketan. Overnight stay in Rishikesh.

Day 8 – Rishikesh Sightseeing & Departure

Visit Beatles Ashram or enjoy leisure time near the river. Optional river rafting (seasonal). Drive back to Delhi. Tour concludes.
