Day-wise Japan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo

Arrive at Tokyo International Airport and transfer to your hotel. Rest of the day is free to recover from travel or explore nearby areas at leisure.

Day 2 – Tokyo City Tour

Full-day city tour covering Asakusa Temple, Nakamise Street, Imperial Palace area (photo stop), Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower vicinity. Evening free for shopping or dining.

Day 3 – Mount Fuji & Hakone Excursion

Scenic excursion to Mount Fuji and Hakone region. Visit Lake Ashi and surrounding viewpoints (subject to weather). Return to Tokyo by evening.

Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto by Bullet Train

Board the Shinkansen bullet train to Kyoto. Afternoon sightseeing includes Fushimi Inari Shrine and Gion district. Overnight in Kyoto.

Day 5 – Kyoto & Nara Exploration

Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Arashiyama Bamboo Grove. Continue to Nara to explore Todaiji Temple and Nara Deer Park. Return to Kyoto by evening.

Day 6 – Kyoto → Hiroshima

Travel by bullet train to Hiroshima. Visit Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome. Evening free to explore the city. Overnight in Hiroshima.

Day 7 – Hiroshima → Osaka

Morning free or optional visit to Miyajima Island (Itsukushima Shrine). Later proceed to Osaka by train. Evening explore Dotonbori district.

Day 8 – Departure from Osaka

After breakfast, transfer to Kansai International Airport for your onward journey. Tour concludes with deep cultural memories of Japan.
