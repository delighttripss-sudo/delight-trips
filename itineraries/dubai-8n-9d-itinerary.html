Day-wise Dubai Luxury Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival & VIP Transfer

Arrive at Dubai International Airport. Meet your luxury concierge and enjoy private transfer to a 5★ hotel. Relax with a welcome drink, spa session or private beach access. Enjoy dinner at a premium rooftop restaurant.

Day 2 – Dubai Private City Tour

Personalized guided city tour with private vehicle covering iconic sights: Burj Al Arab, Jumeirah Mosque, Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis (outside), Gold & Spice Souks, and a luxury lunch at DIFC.

Day 3 – Burj Khalifa VIP + Fountain Lounge

Exclusive Burj Khalifa VIP access to the 148th floor. Premium lounge experience followed by a private fountain show view with sparkling refreshments.

Day 4 – Private Yacht Cruise

Set sail on a private yacht in Dubai Marina with curated dining, sunset views and champagne. Afternoon leisure at JBR Beach or The Walk.

Day 5 – Private Desert Safari & Luxury Camp

Premium desert safari with dune driving, camel ride, private sunset dinner, falconry show and live entertainment at a luxury desert camp with bonfire & gourmet BBQ.

Day 6 – Abu Dhabi Highlights Tour

Guided Abu Dhabi tour including Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Emirates Palace photo stop, Corniche, Louvre Abu Dhabi (optional) and luxury lunch with marina views. Return to Dubai for evening leisure.

Day 7 – Theme Park VIP + Spa Leisure

VIP access to either Dubai Parks & Resorts or Atlantis Aquaventure Waterpark with priority entry. Evening spa treatments or luxury sunset dinner cruise (optional).

Day 8 – Leisure & Shopping

Free day for world-class shopping at Mall of the Emirates or Dubai Mall’s Fashion Avenue, afternoon high tea at Burj Al Arab, or visits to City Walk & La Mer. Evening at your own pace.

Day 9 – Departure

Check-out after breakfast and enjoy private airport transfer with VIP meet-and-assist for your departure. Tour concludes.
