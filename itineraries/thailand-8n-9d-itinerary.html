Day-wise Thailand Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival in Phuket

Arrive at Phuket International Airport. Meet and greet followed by hotel transfer. Check-in and evening free to relax at Patong Beach or explore local markets.

Day 2 – Phi Phi Island Tour

Full-day Phi Phi Island tour by speedboat covering Maya Bay, Monkey Beach, snorkeling points and scenic lagoons. Lunch included. Return to Phuket by evening.

Day 3 – Phang Nga Bay & James Bond Island

Day excursion to Phang Nga Bay featuring limestone cliffs, canoeing through caves, floating village and James Bond Island. Evening return to Phuket.

Day 4 – Phuket → Krabi Transfer

After breakfast, scenic road transfer to Krabi. Hotel check-in and evening free to relax at Ao Nang Beach or enjoy seaside dining.

Day 5 – Krabi Four Island Tour

Speedboat Four Island tour covering Phra Nang Cave, Chicken Island, Tup Island and Poda Island. Snorkeling, beach time and packed lunch included.

Day 6 – Krabi → Chiang Mai (Flight)

Transfer to airport for flight to Chiang Mai. Arrival and hotel check-in. Evening visit to Night Bazaar or leisure time.

Day 7 – Chiang Mai Temples & Mountain Views

Guided Chiang Mai city tour including Wat Phra Singh, Wat Chedi Luang and Doi Suthep Temple with panoramic city views. Evening free or optional cultural show.

Day 8 – Chiang Mai → Bangkok

Transfer to airport for flight to Bangkok. Hotel check-in followed by half-day city tour covering Golden Buddha, Marble Temple and shopping districts.

Day 9 – Departure from Bangkok

After breakfast, transfer to Bangkok International Airport for onward journey. Tour concludes with a complete Thailand experience.
